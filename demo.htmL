1. Executive Summary & Operational Problem

During deep-space missions to the Lunar Gateway or Mars, communication delays with Earth reach 20 to 48 minutes each way and experience multi-day solar conjunction blackouts. EGLE MONITOR is an offline-first, deterministic clinical decision support system that centralizes crew biometrics, cabin life support (ECLSS), EVA suit pressure, and ionizing radiation dosimetry—translating complex aerospace telemetry into plain-English guidance and step-by-step emergency protocols for crew members without medical training.

3. Verified NASA & Clinical Thresholds

NASA-STD-3001 · OCHMO · GERNHARDT · WHITSON
  • Cabin Atmosphere & Hypoxia (OCHMO-TB-003): Normoxia at 101.3 kPa (PIO2 = 149 mmHg, SpO2 97–100%); Artemis exploration habitat at 8.2 psia / 34% O2 (SpO2 94–96%); critical hypoxia alert < 90%.
  • EVA Spacesuit (Gernhardt et al., 2013): EMU suit pressure at 29.6 kPa (4.3 psia, 100% O2). Hypoxia and DCS are prevented via hardware secondary oxygen pressure regulation rather than suit SpO2.
  • Radiation Dosimetry (NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 1): Converts μGy/h to mSv/h via Q(LET); triggers 20s Fast Mode above 0.50 mSv/h and core shelter relocation above 1.0 mSv/h (600 mSv career cap).
  • Renal & Behavioral Health (OCHMO-MTB-003): Urinary calcium alerts (>250 mg/24h female, >300 mg/24h male) with Potassium Citrate (20 mEq/d); ungraded PHQ-9, GAD-7, PSQI & POMS logs queued automatically for Earth psychologists.
